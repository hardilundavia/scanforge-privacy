ScanForge Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version

ScanForge reads and creates codes on your phone. Your scan history, the codes you make, your folders, tags and notes stay on your device. We do not run an account system and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

What stays on your phone

Everything you scan or create, your history, folders, tags, notes, saved styles and settings are stored only on this device. So are your loyalty cards, pantry and shopping list, expiry dates, the prices you enter and your business cards. Deleting the app deletes them. Backups you export are files you control.

Backups

Encrypted backups are protected with a password you choose. A backup file is saved only where you pick (for example iCloud Drive or Google Drive) and cannot be read without the password; we never receive it or the password.

If you turn on daily automatic backup, an encrypted backup is kept in ScanForge’s own folder on your phone, which your phone includes in its iCloud or Google device backup. The password is kept in your phone’s secure Keychain or Keystore so the backup can run by itself. Turning automatic backup off removes the stored password.

Files shared with ScanForge

When you share an image or PDF with ScanForge, or pick one with “From a file”, it is read on your phone to find codes and then deleted. It is not uploaded. Codes found in it are added to your history like any other scan.

Reminders and widgets

Expiry reminders are notifications scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to a server. The “My code” widget shows a picture of the code you choose; that picture is stored on your phone where the widget can read it. On iPhone, the title and type of your latest scan are stored the same way for the “Last scan” widget; the title is hidden on a locked phone when lock-screen data is off.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to read codes and, if you choose Text / OCR, to read printed text. Images are processed on the device and are not uploaded. Photos are read only when you pick one to scan, and saved only when you choose to save a code.

Location

Location is off unless you turn on “Save scan location”. When it is on, the place of a scan is stored with that scan on your phone so it can appear on the scan map. It is not sent to us.

When the app contacts the internet

Product barcodes: the barcode number (or the product number inside GS1 product data) is sent to open product databases (Open Food Facts and its sister projects, Open Prices and UPCitemdb) to look up the product. Book barcodes (ISBN) are looked up on Open Library.

Links: when “Check links before opening” is on, the app loads the link you scanned to check it and show a preview, which contacts that website.

Exchange rates: when a price list is shown, daily exchange rates are downloaded from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) at most once a day so prices from other countries can be shown in your currency. Nothing about you or your scans is sent.

Malware list: a public list of dangerous sites, compiled by abuse.ch URLhaus, is downloaded from the malware-filter project (GitLab, GitHub or Cloudflare Pages) about once a day. Your links are compared with it on your phone and are not sent anywhere for this.

Scan map: map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which shows that service the area being viewed.

These services receive your internet address, as any website does, and handle it under their own policies.

Crash reports and usage statistics

To find and fix problems we use Google Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics. They receive crash details, which screens are opened, the type of code scanned or created (for example “QR link”), and the file format of exports, together with general device information such as model and system version. They never receive the content of a code, links, Wi-Fi passwords, contacts, notes or your location. You can switch this off at any time in Settings › Crash reports & analytics.

App lock

If you turn on “Lock history”, Face ID, fingerprint or your passcode is checked by your phone’s own system. ScanForge never sees or stores your biometric data.

Suggestions for scanned text

Scanned text can get suggestions: an event, a contact, a list or a summary. They are worked out on your phone, by the AI built into newer phones (Apple Intelligence on iPhone, Gemini Nano on Android) or by the app itself on other phones. The text is not sent to us or to anyone else. Nothing is saved until you choose an action.

ScanForge Pro

ScanForge Pro is a subscription through Google Play, which handles the payment and renewals under its own policies. ScanForge never sees your card or account details; it asks Google Play whether a subscription is active and keeps only that answer on your phone. You can cancel at any time in Google Play.

Tips

Tips in “Support ScanForge” are paid through the App Store or Google Play, which handle the payment under their own policies. ScanForge never sees your card or account details; it only keeps the number of tips on your phone to show the supporter badge.

Children

ScanForge is a general utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can turn off link checks, location, automatic backup, reminders and crash reporting in Settings, delete individual items or clear all history, and remove everything by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this policy will be updated in the app and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact details on ScanForge’s store page.
